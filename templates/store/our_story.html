The Soul of Bunai

Honoring Generations of Indian Craftsmanship

In Hindi, "Bunai" (बुनाई) means the sacred art of weaving, knotting, and interlacing threads of craft tradition. We bridge ancient artisan heritage with modern ethical homes.

Direct Artisan Patronage

Every rupee spent on Bunai directly compensates our artisan families, ensuring fair living wages, healthcare stipends, and dignified continuity of heritage craft techniques.

Organic & Sustainable

We use only unbleached raw cotton, natural vegetable indigo dyes, ethically gathered Himachal merino wool, and natural river clay. Completely free of microplastics.

Zero Plastic Philosophy

All our shipments are carefully wrapped in handmade seed paper, recycled corrugated boxes, and natural jute ties that return safely to the earth.

Explore our seasonal drops of handwoven Chanderi sarees, macrame decor, ceramic mugs, and needlecraft.
